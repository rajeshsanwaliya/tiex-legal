Delete your TieX account

App: TieX · Developer: TieX · Contact: support.tiex@gmail.com

Option 1 — delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open TieX and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm twice. Your profile and photos disappear immediately.

You can also choose Deactivate Account instead. That hides your profile right away and permanently deletes everything 30 days later, unless you log back in before then.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it (no app needed)

If you cannot sign in or have already uninstalled the app, email support.tiex@gmail.com from the email address on the account with the subject Delete my TieX account.

Include the email address or phone number you signed up with, so we can find the account. We may ask one question to confirm the account is yours — we do this to stop somebody else from deleting your account. We acknowledge within 24 hours and complete the deletion within 7 business days.

What is deleted

Once the deletion runs, all of the following are permanently erased and cannot be restored:

  • Your profile — name, age, gender, city, bio, interests and preferences
  • All photos and voice notes you uploaded
  • All chat messages, matches, swipes, follows and friend requests
  • Notifications, profile visits, live-stream history, comments and gifts
  • Your login (email or phone) and the account itself
  • Identity-verification documents and payout details, once no payout obligation remains
  • Any unused coin balance and unwithdrawn earnings, which are forfeited

What we keep, and for how long

KeptWhyHow long
Coin purchase, token and payout records (amounts, dates, transaction IDs) Indian financial and tax law requires a record of money received and paid out. Your name and contact details are stripped from these rows — what remains cannot identify you. Up to 8 years
A one-way hash of your email / phone number Lets us recognise an account that was deleted while banned or under investigation, so it cannot quietly return. A hash cannot be turned back into your email or phone number. Indefinitely
Records of a report or ban against the account Keeps other users safe from a banned account returning. As long as needed for safety

How long it takes

  • Immediately — your profile, photos and presence disappear from the app for everyone.
  • Within 10 days — an automatic nightly job permanently erases everything listed above.
  • During those 10 days you can sign back in to cancel the deletion. After that, nothing can be recovered.
Deleting your TieX account does not cancel anything on Google Play. Coins are consumed on purchase and are not refundable through us — for a Play purchase issue, see Google Play refunds, or read the refund policy inside the app under Settings.
