Coins

How paying works.

TieX runs on in-app coins. Calls and gifts are paid for in coins, and coin packs are bought through Google Play — your card details never reach us. Coins are for use inside TieX; they are not money and cannot be cashed out. Creators earn a share of what is spent on them and withdraw in rupees to their own bank account after verification.
