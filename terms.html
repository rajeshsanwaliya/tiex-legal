5. Coins (Tokens) & Payments

TieX uses a virtual coin system for paid features. Important clarifications:

  • Coins are purchased in packs via Google Play Billing (UPI, cards, Play balance)

  • Coins do not expire as long as your account is active

VIP Subscription: One-time purchase, non-transferable, not auto-renewed.

Refunds: Coin purchases are final and non-refundable once coins are credited, except for duplicate charges, technical failures where the service was not delivered, or unauthorised transactions. See the Refund Policy in Settings for full details.
