🔒 TieX — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 8, 2026

TieX ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a TieX account, we collect:

  • Email address (used for login and account recovery)
  • Name, age, gender, city, country, and profile bio
  • Profile photos you upload
  • Interests, vibe tags, and preferences you choose
  • Approximate location (country/region) — to show nearby and same-country profiles and apply local rules
  • Device info (OS version, device model) for support and crash reports

During your use of the app, we also collect:

  • Swipe history (who you liked or passed)
  • Chat messages (stored securely in our database)
  • Online / activity status (your "last active" time, shown as an online indicator)
  • Token/coin purchase history and wallet balance
  • App usage patterns (screens visited, session duration)
  • Push notification token (to deliver match, message, call and follow alerts)

For creators (users who choose to earn) we additionally collect KYC / identity data — government ID type and number, ID document photo, date of birth, and a short verification selfie/video — and payout details (UPI ID or bank account number, IFSC, account holder name). These are used only to verify you are a real adult and to process withdrawals.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Matching & Discovery — your profile is shown to compatible users based on preferences, country, and shared interests
  • Location — your approximate location personalises Discovery and enforces region-specific rules; we do not continuously track precise GPS
  • Presence — your last-active time powers the online indicator
  • Token System — purchases, deductions, and balances enable paid features (chat, voice, video)
  • Notifications — match, message, call, follow, recharge, and reminder alerts
  • Safety & Moderation — messages and profiles are scanned by automated systems for harmful or fake content
  • Smart Features — limited text may be processed in real time by a third-party AI provider to translate chats and suggest replies

We do NOT sell your data, use it for advertising on other platforms, or share your photos, ID, or payout details with other users.

3. Identity Verification (KYC) — Creators

Creators who wish to withdraw earnings must complete identity verification (government ID, ID number, date of birth, and a short selfie/video).

  • Purpose — confirm you are a real person aged 18+, prevent fraud, and meet payout/financial-compliance rules
  • Access — KYC data and verification videos are visible ONLY to our authorised review/admin team, never to other users
  • Storage — stored encrypted; ID numbers are treated as sensitive personal data
  • Retention — kept while your creator account is active and for the period required by financial/tax law after your last payout, then deleted

4. Payments & Payout Data

Purchases: Payments are processed by our payment provider on its own secure page — TieX never sees or stores your payment credentials. We store only the amount paid, pack purchased, transaction ID, and date.

Payouts (creators): To pay your earnings we store the UPI ID or bank details you enter. These are used solely to send your money and are visible only to our payout/admin team. We may share limited payout and tax information with payment partners and tax authorities where the law requires.

5. Smart Features & AI Processing

TieX uses automated technology, including a third-party AI provider (Groq), to enhance your experience — chat translation, optional reply/conversation suggestions, profile-setup help, and safety moderation. Only the text needed for the feature is processed, in real time, and this may occur on servers outside your country (see Section 9). Your content is NOT used to train any AI model, and the smart features are optional.

6. Calls Are Not Recorded

Voice and video calls are transmitted in real time through our calling provider (Agora) and are NOT recorded or stored by TieX. We also block screenshots and screen recording during calls and on sensitive screens. We keep only the metadata needed for billing and safety (e.g. call duration, participants, tokens spent).

7. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: data retained while you use TieX
  • Deactivated accounts: retained for 30 days, then deleted
  • Deleted accounts: personal data deleted within 7 business days
  • Chat messages: deleted when both users delete the conversation, or at account deletion
  • Token & payout transaction logs: retained up to 8 years for financial/tax compliance
  • KYC records: retained as required by financial law after last payout, then deleted
  • Crash/error logs: retained for 90 days

8. Your Rights

Wherever you live, you can access, correct, delete, or export your data, and withdraw consent (by deleting your account from Settings → Delete Account).

EU / EEA / UK (GDPR): our legal bases are your consent, performance of our contract with you, and our legitimate interests in safety and fraud prevention. You may object to or restrict processing and lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): you may know, delete, and correct your information and opt out of "sale"/"sharing" — note we do not sell your personal information.

To exercise any right, contact our Grievance/Privacy Officer: Rajesh Sanwaliya — support.tiex@gmail.com (response within 30 days).

9. International Data Transfers

TieX serves users in many countries. To run the service, your data may be stored or processed on servers outside your own country (our database, AI, calling, and push providers may operate in India, the United States, or the EU). Where required we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Cookies & Tracking

TieX uses minimal local storage and SDKs needed to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand basic app usage and crashes. We do not use third-party advertising trackers. Where local law requires, we ask for your consent before any non-essential tracking, and you can decline.

11. Data Security

We protect your data using HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, Row-Level Security so users can only access their own data, encrypted storage for sensitive fields (ID, payout details), and server-side secrets that never ship inside the app. No system is 100% secure — if you suspect unauthorised access, contact support.tiex@gmail.com immediately.

12. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage)
  • Razorpay / app-store billing (payment processing)
  • Agora (real-time voice and video calls)
  • Groq (AI text translation & suggestions)
  • Expo (app infrastructure and push notifications)

Each third party has its own privacy policy. TieX is not responsible for their practices beyond our contracts with them.

13. Children's Privacy

TieX is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, email support.tiex@gmail.com and we will delete it immediately.

14. Governing Law & Grievances

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act 2000, the SPDI Rules 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. Disputes are subject to the courts of Gujarat, India. Nothing here removes mandatory data-protection or consumer rights you have under the laws of your own country.

Grievance Officer (IT Rules 2021): Rajesh Sanwaliya — support.tiex@gmail.com. Acknowledged within 24 hours, resolved within 30 days.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date and, for significant changes, send an in-app or push alert. Continued use of TieX after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions? support.tiex@gmail.com
